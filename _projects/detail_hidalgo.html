HIDALGO: Spanish humanoid robot project at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM), aiming to build the lightest and lowest-cost humanoid possible. Led by Miguel Hernando, the project grew out of the HackLab UPM-BQ maker lab (co-founded with Alberto Valero, BQ Education), of which I was a member during my B.Sc. Development was organised in subsystem teams: head (2 eyes, 3-DOF neck), hip and upper leg, arms/shoulders and 3D-printed hand, and lower leg/foot. [project blog]
