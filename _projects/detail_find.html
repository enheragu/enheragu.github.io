Interdisciplinary collaboration with the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid (UAM) that builds on ACTVIS. The project studies how locomotion affects visual search in natural environments, and whether higher motor complexity improves the retention of information while also increasing distraction. It also explores individual differences in the propensity to distraction, and whether training with a differential outcomes procedure can reduce the interference of distractors.
